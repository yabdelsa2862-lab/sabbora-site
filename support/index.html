Questions, a bug, or an idea? Report it here and you'll hear back.

Notes aren't being written for long lectures.
Short lectures are summarised on your iPhone. For long ones, add an AI key in Settings → API Keys (Google Gemini has a free tier), and pick the model under Settings → Notes Model.
How do I record Egyptian Arabic?
Tap the language button on the Record card and choose Egyptian Arabic. The speech model (530 MB) downloads the first time — Wi-Fi is best.
Can I start recording without opening the app?
Yes. Add “Record a Lecture” from the Control Center gallery, or set it on the Action Button. You can also record from the Sabbora app on Apple Watch.
Where do my recordings go?
They stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent, and only when you choose.
