Sabbora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Sabbora is made by Mohamed Abdelsalam. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of its own.

What stays on your device

Your recordings, transcripts, notes, board photos and quizzes are stored on your iPhone (and on your Apple Watch while a recording is being sent to your iPhone). Audio is turned into text on your device and is never uploaded. You can delete any lecture, and kept audio is deleted automatically after the number of days you choose in Settings.

What leaves your device — only if you choose

If you add an API key for an AI provider (such as Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or Microsoft Azure), the text of a lecture is sent to that provider to write your notes, study guides, quizzes and answers, and board photos are sent to be read. This goes directly from your device to the provider you chose, under that provider's own privacy policy, using your own key. We never receive it. You can turn this off at any time with “Keep recordings on this device” in Settings.

Other services

  • Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device speech services, or an on-device Whisper model for Egyptian Arabic, downloaded from Apple.
  • Music recognition uses Apple's ShazamKit, which receives a short audio signature, not the recording.
  • If you allow calendar access, your calendar is read on your device to show your next class. It is not sent anywhere.

Children

Sabbora is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions? Visit Sabbora Support.
